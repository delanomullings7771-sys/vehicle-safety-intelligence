Vehicle Safety Intelligence

NHTSA crash records and owner complaints, combined for human-reviewed safety screening

One question, two kinds of evidence

Which vehicles and components deserve a closer safety look, and how serious are the incidents behind them? This tool brings together structured police-reported crash data (CRSS 2020–2024) and unstructured owner-complaint narratives (NHTSA ODI, 1995–2026), linked at make, model and model year.

Complaint triage

Reads a narrative, recommends the affected component groups and flags reports describing a crash, fire, injury or death.

Crash analysis

Scores a police crash record (CRSS format) for injury and for serious or fatal injury, shows the fields behind each score, and compares it with the outcome the police recorded.

Vehicle lookup

Sets complaint volume against CRSS-estimated crash involvement (a proxy for exposure), so vehicles are compared fairly rather than by raw counts.
